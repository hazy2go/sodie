Sodie Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Who we are

Sodie is a moderation and community bot run by the SODAX community team for one Discord server: the official SODAX server. It is not a public bot and can't be added to other servers. This policy explains what Sodie collects there, why, how long it keeps it and how to reach us.

What Sodie collects and why

  • Member information (Discord user ID, username, display name, join date, roles). Used to verify new members with a captcha, detect join raids and staff-impersonation names, give roles, and log joins and leaves for moderators.
  • Message content in channels Sodie can see. Checked as it's posted for spam, phishing links, scam patterns, mass mentions and unauthorised invite or OAuth links. Most messages are checked and then forgotten; Sodie does not keep a copy of normal chat.
  • Flagged messages. When a message looks like a scam, its text is kept in a moderator review queue until staff handle the alert.
  • Support tickets. When a ticket is archived, Sodie saves its messages and attachments as a transcript so staff can restore the ticket or look back at a case.
  • Moderation records. Warnings, bans, verification status and security log entries.

How long we keep it

DataKept for
Security log entries90 days
Warnings180 days
Handled scam alerts30 days
Ticket transcriptsUntil staff delete them, or on request
Normal chat messagesNot stored

Everything is stored in a private database on a server the SODAX team controls. If Sodie leaves the server, that server's data is deleted.

Who we share it with

We never sell data or share it for advertising. Data leaves Sodie's own server only in these cases:

  • Discord, which hosts the conversations Sodie works in (see Discord's own privacy policy).
  • Auto-translation. In channels where staff turn translation on, the message text is sent to an AI translation service (NVIDIA) to produce the translation.
  • AI scam review. Optionally, the text of a message already flagged as a possible scam may be sent to an AI model (Anthropic) for a second opinion for moderators.

Your messages and data are never used to train any AI or machine-learning model. The services above only return one answer per request.

Your choices

Scam and spam protection applies to everyone in the server, so you can't opt out of message checks while you are a member. Leaving the server stops all collection. You can ask us to see or delete data Sodie holds about you, including ticket transcripts, except where we need to keep a record of a ban to protect the community.

Contact

Open a support ticket in the SODAX Discord server and ask for the privacy team. We aim to answer within 30 days.

Changes

If we change how Sodie handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
